Rich Text Chatter Tool

The ultimate communication powerhouse for Odoo. Professional formatting, native document previews, pinned messages, and incredibly fast canned responses in one elegant suite.

WYSIWYG Formatting

Complete rich text formatting toolbar. Send messages with proper structure (bold, italics, lists, and links) directly from the chatter feed without opening full windows.

Native (/) Commands

Type "/" to trigger Odoo's native command menu directly in the chatter. Insert tables, media, and structural blocks seamlessly as if you were in the Website Editor.

Interactive Previews

Don't litter your downloads folder. Click on any DOCX, XLSX, or PDF file attachment to view its contents natively immediately over the interface.

Pin Important Messages

Pin crucial agreements, instructions, or files strictly at the top of the chat thread. A simple visual icon indicates what is pinned for everyone.

Instant Quote & Reply

Keep conversations structured in crowded threads. Click "Quote" on any past message to instantly append it as beautiful blockquote in your new reply.

HTML Canned Responses

Accelerate your workflow with pre-configured Rich HTML shortcuts. Filter them precisely to ensure they appear only in Log Notes or Messages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is it compatible with Odoo Enterprise?

Yes! The module enhances the native chatter equally in both Enterprise and Community editions.

How are Canned Responses categorized?

Odoo handles them globally by default, but our module lets you restrict them to only show for 'Log Notes' or 'Send Messages' to keep your menus clean.

Does it support the Mobile App?

Yes. While advanced rich-text composition is primarily optimized for browser setups, the Mobile App respects all generated HTML formatting faithfully on read.

Will it alter my existing messages or database?

Absolutely not. Your historical data is 100% safe. The module strictly upgrades the visual rendering engine and the composition interface going forward.
